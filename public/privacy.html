Privacy Policy

Bone Geek app · Bone Typing · Bone Geek Random Defense — effective 2026-11-07

What we collect

  • Ranking records (only when you play a ranked round in Bone Typing): the nickname you type, mode, score, speed, accuracy and time. Stored to show the global leaderboard.
  • Nickname key: a random code made on your device so nobody else can use your nickname.
  • Button counts: how many times share/follow buttons were tapped — counts only, not who tapped.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. There are no accounts, ads or trackers.

Stays on your device

Game progress (best scores, codex, settings), records from offline play and the "Today's bone" reminder settings stay on your device and are never sent to a server. Reminders are scheduled on the device.

Storage

Ranking records are stored with Supabase (Seoul region); the websites are hosted on Vercel. Records are kept while the leaderboard is running.

Deletion

Send your nickname and recovery code by DM to @iamnotwoodz on Instagram and we will delete that nickname's records. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
